What it does

Built around one idea

Ending a sentence is the whole gesture

No buttons and no second column. The sentence you just finished is replaced where it sits, and the cursor stays exactly where you left it.

Twelve languages, set up properly

Urdu, Punjabi, Arabic and Persian switch the page to right-to-left with the right typeface and the line spacing those scripts need.

Your own words are never lost

Every change is recorded. Open the history to see what you first typed, or undo the last change in a single click.

Where it lives

Your writing stays yours

On your device by default

Entries are stored in this browser. Without an account, nothing is uploaded and nothing is sent anywhere.

Locked if you want it locked

Set a four-digit PIN for the whole diary, or lock individual entries. The PIN is stored as a hash, never as the digits themselves.

Synced only if you ask

Create an account and your entries follow you between devices. The database refuses to return anyone else's rows.
